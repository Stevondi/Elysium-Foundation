WHO WE ARE

Small beginnings.
Bigger possibilities.

Great change does not always begin with great resources. Sometimes, it begins with a small contribution, a shared idea, and the determination to keep going.

Elysium Foundation brings people together through collective savings, small business initiatives, mentorship and responsible financial opportunities to build a stronger culture of self-reliance.

WHY ELYSIUM

A name inspired by a better tomorrow.

Elysium refers to a state of perfect happiness and fulfillment. For us, it represents more than a name — it represents an aspiration.

We envision communities where people have the confidence, resources and opportunities to build better lives.

OUR STORY

Born from a simple idea. Built for a better future.

On July 31, 2023, Elysium Foundation was founded by Steven Omondi and Abraham Omondi with a vision of encouraging youth financial growth and self-reliance.

What began as an initiative among young people grew from the belief that discipline, collaboration and small financial contributions can create greater opportunities.

Our journey is shaped by self-motivation, peer mentorship and perseverance through early challenges.
